BTC when I measured: **64,006**.

Not in the range they gave. And it sits **0.01%** above the upper edge of their own invalidation level — right on the line.

That rule is not a future event. It is triggering as you read this.

THE QUESTION NOBODY ASKS: WHICH GEOMETRY PAYS?

Scoring one specific plan only answers "does this plan pay". The more useful question: **on this asset's own history, does any geometry pay?**

So I scanned the grid. 6 stop distances (in units of the asset's own daily range) × 5 reward ratios × 3 horizons = **90 cells per asset**, each one walked bar by bar.

The danger has to be named straight away: **testing 90 cells and showing off the best one is a con.** The best cell always exists, even when everything is noise. So I report three numbers together: cells tested, cells positive, and the median cell.

```
                              $ENA        $ICP
cells tested                    90          90
cells positive                   0           8
median cell                -0.098R     -0.094R
best cell                   0.000R      0.157R
```

**ENA: not one of 90 cells has positive expectancy.** The best cell is exactly 0.000R — dead break-even, and that is the best after trying everything. This is not "no good entry found yet". On its own history, there is no entry at all.

ICP has 8 positive cells out of 90. Few. But they are **not scattered** — all 8 sit in the same corner:

```
                              stop      horizon
0.026R  R:R 5.0            2.5 ATR      90 days
0.107R  R:R 5.0            3.0 ATR      90 days
0.019R  R:R 1.5            4.0 ATR      60 days
0.025R  R:R 1.5            4.0 ATR      90 days
```

Every positive cell uses a **wide** stop and a **long** horizon. Not one has a stop below 2.5 daily ranges. That is exactly the shape every measurement this week has pointed at: tight stops die of noise, not of being wrong about direction.

Clustering in one place is **weak** evidence of real structure — not sufficient evidence. 8 cells out of 90, each on roughly 10 independent episodes. I am reporting it because the shape means something, not because the number is enough.

ICP's best cell, converted to prices from here:

```
entry                       2.3650
stop                        2.0070     −15.14%
target                      3.4389     +45.41%
hit rate                     27.4%   needs 25.0%
expectancy                  0.157R      90 days
```

That stop is 15.14% wide. It sounds frightening, and it is — but ICP's median weekly range is 19.60%. To hold for three months the stop has to be wider than an ordinary week, or you are simply paying for an ordinary week.

In capital terms that means the position must be **much smaller**. The same risk spread across an honest distance.
