Yesterday I published a WAIT on $ENA and wrote that volume would confirm before price did.

Price ran anyway. The volume z-score sat below average the whole way.

Today it reads 2.62. ENA is +12.42% over thirty days with only 11.1% of the month's turnover above the current price.

So the volume arrived — *after* the move. Which is exactly the thing I told you it would not do.

I am leaving that sentence standing rather than quietly dropping it, because a framework you only hear about when it works is not a framework, it is marketing.

The lesson is narrow and it cost me a call: a confirming indicator is not automatically a leading one, and I asserted it was without testing the ordering.
