Someone just rebuilt their altcoin screen around exactly what I have been measuring these past few days, re-ran it, and sent me the result to check.

Their new rules, copied verbatim:

```
volume trend          must be positive
30-day range position above 85% disqualifies Early
supply trapped above  above 35% penalised, above 50% usually excluded
geometry              prefer positive expectancy across MANY combinations
```

**All four rules are right.** I am not going to hedge here: these are exactly the things that need to be in a screen, and very few scans are willing to write them down as hard thresholds.

Then I applied them with real numbers. Nothing passes — and the name that fails worst is sitting at number one.

THE SCORECARD

```
                         $SUI (#1)
volume                      -41.0%         fail
trapped supply               78.9%         fail
30-day range                 21.2%         pass
positive geometry cells       6/90         fail
```

SUI's trapped supply: **78.9%**.

Their own rule says above 50% is excluded. Their table records this field as **"moderate"**.

SUI's volume is -41.0%. The table says **"fine"**.

Failing four gates out of five, ranked first on all three boards.

AND THIS IS A DIAGNOSIS, NOT A COMPLAINT

Look closely at what is right and what is wrong and a very clean pattern appears:

```
                        table said     measured
$ENA trapped supply       high ~35%       32.9%
$ENA beta                 high 1.5+        1.52
$ICP trapped supply             low       0.00%
$ICP volume                  strong       71.7%
$ICP range                very high       93.2%
$SUI trapped supply        moderate       78.9%
$SUI volume                    fine      -41.0%
```

**Every ENA field is right. Every ICP field is right. Every SUI field is wrong.**

Not because they were careless with SUI. Because ENA and ICP **have numbers** — I measured and published them days ago, and they picked them up. Nobody has measured SUI, so it entered the table as **adjectives**.

And here is the sentence I want you to take away:

**A gate that reads "above 50% is excluded" does nothing when the input to that field is the word "moderate".**

The screen is not broken. It has not been given numbers to run on.

That is the kind of fault that survives every round of rule improvement, because looking at the table you see columns, thresholds and scores. Only when you replace words with numbers do you find out whether that gate has ever closed.

THE RULE THAT EMPTIES THE WHOLE LIST

Their fourth rule is my favourite: *prefer setups with positive expectancy across many stop and reward-ratio combinations, rather than showing off one best cell.*

I scanned 90 combinations per asset — 6 stop distances in units of the asset's own daily range, 5 reward ratios, 3 horizons, each cell walked bar by bar.

```
                       positive cells    median cell
$SUI                             6/90        -0.091R
$ENA                             0/90        -0.101R
$ICP                             8/90        -0.096R
```

ENA: **not one of 90**. The median cell is negative for all three.

That rule, run honestly, wipes out the list. And I think that is the **correct result** for this market right now, rather than a failure of the rule. A good screen has to be allowed to return zero.

ONE MORE THING BACKWARDS

They penalise "high beta" because BTC is weak — reasonable. But they penalise the wrong name.

```
                              beta    BTC explains
$SUI                          1.19           64.0%
$ENA                          1.52           30.5%
$ICP                          0.64           17.0%
```

ENA has the higher beta, but **SUI is the one welded to BTC** — BTC explains 64.0% of its daily variance, double ENA and nearly four times ICP.

Beta says *how far it travels*; squared correlation says *how much of it is BTC's doing*. In a "penalise when BTC is weak" regime, the second is the one to penalise. SUI most deserves the penalty and is sitting at the top of the board.

WHAT THEY GOT RIGHT

This has to be said in full, because this part is the part worth copying.

**ICP was pushed out of Early.** Correct. 30-day range position 93.2%, stage 2 expansion — no case for calling that early.

**And they predicted the consequence correctly too:** *"nice structure but it has already run → expectancy falls."* I have a number for that. Take every past ICP day with hot volume **and** a high position in the range — the exact state it is in now — and walk forward 30 days:

```
finished higher            6.25%
baseline, same bar count   38.5%
difference           -32.3 points
```

Median return -20.4%.

But once the overlap is removed only 0.5 independent episodes remain, which is **-0.48 sigma** — nothing proven. I am including it because every horizon beyond five days points the same way, not because the number is sufficient.

BTC at 64,060 does fall inside the range they gave this time. Credit where it is due.

ONE MORE GATE WORTH ADDING

The cheapest of them all:

**No name gets scored if any field is an adjective.** Volume, trapped supply, range position, beta — those four must be a number or blank. If blank, the name does not enter the table rather than being guessed at.

Those four numbers come from public candles in a single call. The price of guessing them is today's board: a name failing four gates out of five sitting in first place.
