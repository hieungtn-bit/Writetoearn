This set of funding thresholds gets copied everywhere: "healthy" is -0.005% to 0.01% per 8 hours, above 0.015% means longs are crowded, above 0.04% is extreme.

I measured it across 279 $BTC funding periods. Two of the four thresholds **have never happened once**.

Funding on this venue is hard-capped at 0.0100%. The 95th percentile equals the 99th equals the maximum — the signature of a ceiling, not of a calm market. So 0.015% and 0.04% sit outside the reachable range entirely.

The "healthy" band has the opposite problem: it spans the 4.30th to the 93.55th percentile. It contains almost the whole distribution. It describes what is normal; it diagnoses nothing.

Today's reading, the one everybody calls "mildly positive", sits at the **54.12nd percentile**.

To be fair to it: the idea behind the thresholds points **the right way**. When funding pins to the cap, forward returns are weaker across all three horizons — -0.86 points after one day, -2.06 after three, -2.43 after seven. None of them clears two sigma, and I only have three months of data.

And 22.22% of periods are negative — shorts paying longs.

Bias: WAIT. A threshold that will not say where it came from is not a measurement. It is a number that sounds right.
