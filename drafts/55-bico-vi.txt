My system flagged $BICO two days ago at $0.01962. It is $0.02465 now, up 25.6%.

I scored it a MISS.

Every alert carries a twelve-hour clock. Inside those twelve hours BICO rose 1.17% and then fell 16.41%. The move everyone can see now arrived after the window closed. Anyone who took the alert with a sane stop was shaken out long before.

Right coin, wrong moment is still wrong — it just comes with a better story.

The part that works: BICO traded $2.2 million in one hour against a normal hour of $111 thousand. 19.8 times. The money arrives first, the crowd arrives after.

The real record: 32 alerts with a closed window, 18.8% hit. Drop the names that only moved because the exchange announced a delisting and it is 11.1% on 27.

The best number on my board, up 93.9%, is one of those. That is not a win. That is a queue forming at the exit.

Bias: WAIT. A machine that finds movement is not a machine that finds profit.
