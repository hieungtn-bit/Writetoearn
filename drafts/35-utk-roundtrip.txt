$UTK printed 0.0244 this morning. It is 0.00795 now. Do you know how long that took?

The 02:00 UTC hour ran 178.1% on its own — $4.912M of turnover in sixty minutes, against a 30-day average of $0.444M for an entire day.

Then 8 of the 10 hours since have closed red.

Today's full-day turnover is $9.995M. That is a volume z-score of 19.13 — an extreme this pair has not seen in its recent history. Nearly all of it traded on the way up and on the way back down.

The high was 0.0244. The low was 0.00703. Price now sits at 0.00795, near the bottom of that range.

Anyone who saw this trending and bought the excitement bought someone else's exit. That is not bad luck, it is the mechanism: a move that needs 23 times normal volume to happen is a move that needs 23 times normal volume to hold, and there is nobody left to supply it.

Bias: WAIT. A spike that round-trips on record turnover is distribution, not accumulation. Chasing what already happened is how the bill arrives.
