The backtest promised that my hourly volume alerts reach +10% within 12 hours 28.72% of the time.

The machine has now run live. 170 alerts with a closed window. The score:

**23.46%** across 162 clean trades.

That is 5.26 points below the backtest. Exactly as every backtest goes: live is worse than paper.

But it is still **3.56 times** a random hour (6.59%). The edge is real. It is just smaller than the brochure.

Three things come with that number.

**One.** 8 alerts are still open and **are not counted**. A position that has not run out its window is not a win.

**Two.** I removed 8 alerts born from delisting news — only 4.49% of the total, but the hardest pumps on the board. Removing them **lowered** the score from 25.88% to 23.46%. I removed them anyway: forced liquidation is the easiest way to manufacture volume, and it says the least about demand.

**Three, and this is the one that matters:** more than three quarters of the alerts **never reach the target**. That is the true shape of a small edge. Anyone selling you a seven-in-ten win rate is selling you something else.

Bias: WAIT. I would rather have 23.46% measured on real money than 28.72% measured on the past.
