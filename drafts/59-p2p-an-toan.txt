Seven checks before you press "Release Coin" on Binance P2P. I keep this taped beside my screen.

1. The money must be IN the account. Open your banking app and look at the real balance. A screenshot is not evidence — it is the easiest thing in the world to fake.

2. The sender's name must MATCH your counterparty's KYC name on Binance. One letter different, you stop. This is the most skipped step of all.

3. Never accept third-party payment. Money from an unfamiliar account means you may be receiving dirty money.

4. Chat only inside Binance P2P. Anyone pulling you onto Telegram or Zalo "to go faster" — open a dispute. Off the platform is outside the protection.

5. Rushing is a signal. Scammers always manufacture urgency. You do not owe anyone your haste.

6. The chargeback trap: the buyer transfers, you release the coin, then within 72 hours they tell the bank "I did not authorise this transaction" and the bank claws it back. The coin is gone and so is the money.

7. If you have doubts, open a dispute with evidence. Do not negotiate it yourself.

The first six steps take under a minute. The seventh saves a month's salary.
