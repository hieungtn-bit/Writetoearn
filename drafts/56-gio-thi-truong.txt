Is $BTC going up or down tonight? I do not know. And I can measure that I do not know.

17,518 hourly bars, two years, bucketed by Vietnam time.

The evening session (19:00–24:00) closes green 48.89% of hours. The whole evening block closes higher 47.95% of sessions. A coin flip. Standard deviation -0.63 — which is nothing.

This is where it nearly caught me. Test 24 hours against one benchmark and one of them will look "significant" no matter what. The best cell on the board reached 2.79 sigma — exactly the noise you manufacture by testing 24 times. Had I not fixed the session blocks BEFORE running it, I would have published a very convincing golden hour that was entirely false.

But there is something real here, and it is large: the range.

The evening session moves 0.71% per hour against 0.48% in the day. That gap is 23.57 sigma. 21:00 is the wildest hour of the day at 0.94%, more than double midday.

Usable today: half of all evening sessions range between 1.13% and 2.61%. A stop tighter than 1.74% gets taken out by a completely ordinary night, with no news required.

Bias: WAIT. The hour of day says nothing about direction and a great deal about size. This is a position-sizing fact, not a side to take.
