THE RARE NUMBER

That is not rounding. Nobody who bought BNB in the past month is underwater.

PRICE STRUCTURE

Price is above both moving averages — SMA20 at 583.44, SMA50 at 575.49 — and the SMA20 is above the SMA50. That is an uptrend structure, not a bounce inside a downtrend.

The 25-day base is 9.73% wide and price sits 1.18% below its top. Tight, and close.

RSI 64.9. ATR 2.11% per day — low volatility.

THREE NUMBERS AGAINST

**One.** Position in the 30-day range: 87.2%. You would be buying near the top of the month, not near the bottom.

**Two.** Volume trend -25.3%. The last three days traded a quarter less than the period before. Price is rising while participation leaves — that is the thing I least like to see at the top of a range.

**Three.** 30-day money flow is 1.37 (more money on up days), but the 90-day is still 0.78 — below 1. The turn is only a month old.

WHAT NOBODY SAYS ABOUT BNB

24-hour volume divided by market cap: **0.64%**.

This week I measured ICP at 3.78% and AVAX at 3.66%. BNB turns over less than a fifth of that rate.

BNB is **held**, not **traded**. That cuts both ways: less supply pressing on every rally, but also less depth to get out through when you need it.

Market cap $80.5 billion, rank 4. Down 55.9% from its all-time high (2025-10-13). Down 24.4% over the past year. 33.4% of max supply has never circulated.

THE MISTAKE MOST WEEKLY PLANS MAKE

This is the most important part of this post.

I measured 104 completed weeks of BNB. One-week range, high to low:

  p25       6.73%
  median   10.01%
  p75      14.60%
  p90      20.31%

The standard stop derived from daily ATR is 3.17%.

**A median week travels 3.16 times further than that stop.**

Setting a stop from daily ATR and then holding for a week is not risk management. It is a guarantee of being swept by a completely ordinary week, with no news required.

AND HERE IS WHY I AM NOT EXCITED

Across those 104 weeks, BNB closed higher 51.0% of them. The median weekly return is 0.07%.

A coin flip. A beautiful structure does not change that.

On top of that, I measured across 17,518 hourly bars that the hour of day says nothing about direction but a great deal about size — the evening block's range sits 23.57 sigma away from the rest. If you hold a leveraged position through 21:00 Vietnam time without watching, that is a choice, not an accident.

Bias: WAIT. BNB's structure is the cleanest thing I measured in the large-cap group this week — no trapped supply, above both moving averages, a tight base, cooling funding. But volume is draining at the top of the range, the 90-day money flow has not turned, and a week is still a coin flip. I want volume to confirm before I call this more than a pretty structure.
